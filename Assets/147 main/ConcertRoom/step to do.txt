Unity setup steps (straightforward):

1. Drag ConcertRoom_WithTable.fbx into the scene
2. Set the Skybox to Dreamy_OLED_HDRI.exr (same as before)
3. Create an Empty GameObject named "ConcertSmoke" and place it near floor level in the center of the room
4. Attach DriftingConcertSmoke.cs to that Empty GameObject (Unity will auto-add the Particle System component)
5. Drag SmokeParticle_Texture.png into the Smoke Texture slot in the Inspector
6. Press Play — smoke will drift upward slowly with gentle swaying, like real air currents, looping continuously

Tune from the Inspector without modifying code: smoke density, drift speed, size, color, opacity — if you want denser/thinner smoke or a different color after testing, just let me know.
